Questions to HEDS, Answered by HEDS

The answers below are taken from the current HEDS website.

What is HEDS?

HEDS is an operating practice led by Daniel Looper. Heavy Equipment Dealer Solutions LLC serves construction, agricultural, truck, forklift and crane rental dealers, municipal and utility fleets, and private equity and search fund buyers of those businesses, across the United States. The entry offer is the Operational Health Analysis at $1,600. Training remains available as a supporting offer.

Is the training onsite?

Yes. HEDS comes to your dealership or rental company, walks the shop, and uses your actual reports. It is not a webinar, and it is not self-paced videos. Training uses your real data, on your floor, with your numbers.

What is the HEDS Industry Knowledge Assessment?

It is a 50-question proctored assessment that measures operating discipline for heavy equipment dealer and rental company managers. It lasts 90 minutes and is administered in person or through a monitored remote session. There are no open books and no AI assistants. It is step one of every HEDS training cycle, so HEDS knows where to focus with each student. It is included in the 5-day course and is not sold on its own.

Why is the assessment proctored?

An unproctored online assessment measures how well someone uses search engines and AI tools. A proctored assessment measures what they actually know when the system is down and the customer is on hold. Proctored means the score is real, so the gaps it reveals are real.

Who should get in touch?

The site is written for Service Managers, Service Advisors, Shop Foremen, Service Administrators, Field Service Coordinators, Dispatch, Parts Managers, Rental Managers, Fleet Managers, Branch Managers, and Regional Managers at heavy equipment dealerships and rental companies, and for anyone responsible for the operating numbers on the floor.
