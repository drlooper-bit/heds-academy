Rental Manager

The Rental dashboard is the commercial view of the fleet: time and dollar utilization, rate realization, downtime, damage recovery, and maintenance cost as a percent of original equipment cost. Rental books labor, parts, and maintenance differently than a dealership, so dealer targets are not applied here. The measures below are taught on the dashboard, and the HEDS targets are covered in training.

KPIFormulaHEDS Target
Time UtilizationDays on rent divided by available rentable days, times 100Covered in training
Dollar UtilizationAnnual rental revenue divided by total original equipment cost (OEC), times 100Covered in training
Maintenance Cost as a Percent of OECAnnual maintenance cost, with technician labor left out, divided by fleet OEC, times 100Covered in training
Equipment DowntimeDown days divided by available days, times 100. Downtime lost revenue is downtime revenue times the cat class time utilization.Covered in training
Rate RealizationActual invoiced rate divided by rack rate, times 100Covered in training
Damage Recovery RateCustomer damage collected divided by total cost of customer damage repairs, times 100Covered in training
Equipment to Orders RatioAvailable units in a class divided by active rental requests for that classCovered in training
EBITDA MarginEBITDA divided by total rental revenue, times 100Covered in training
Customer Churn (quarterly)Customers lost this quarter divided by active customers last quarter, times 100Covered in training

Rental Service Manager

The Rental Service dashboard is the shop that keeps the fleet rent-ready: off-rent turnaround, availability, downtime, damage recovery, and the 48-hour close on rental work orders. Those measures are taught on the dashboard, and the HEDS targets are covered in training.

KPIFormulaHEDS Target
Off Rent to Rent-ReadyHours from off rent into service to the rent-ready line, or to a failed inspectionCovered in training
Labor RecoveryHours on work orders divided by paid payroll hours, times 100Covered in training
Labor UtilizationHours invoiced on work orders divided by hours on work orders, times 100Covered in training
Maintenance Cost as a Percent of OECAnnual maintenance cost, with technician labor left out, divided by fleet OEC, times 100Covered in training
Fleet Physical Availability(Rent-ready units plus on-rent units) divided by total fleet units, times 100Covered in training
Fleet DowntimeDown days divided by total fleet days, times 100. Downtime lost revenue is downtime revenue times the cat class time utilization.Covered in training
Late Close Work OrdersCount of work orders not closed and invoiced within 48 hours of last laborCovered in training
Parts Buffer (Months of Supply)Parts on hand divided by average monthly usage over the last six monthsCovered in training
Damage Recovery RateCustomer damage collected divided by total cost of customer damage repairs, times 100Covered in training
Telematics TriageCritical alerts triaged within 2 hours divided by total critical alerts, times 100Covered in training
