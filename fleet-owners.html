HEDS for Fleet Owners

You own the heavy equipment. Your dealers and rental companies keep it running. HEDS helps you read their service and parts performance with the same KPIs we teach dealer service, parts and rental managers.

Who This Is For

This is for construction companies, municipal fleets and utility fleets that own heavy equipment, depend on dealers and rental companies for parts and service, and need engine and fleet support.

If your machines spend time waiting on a shop, a part or a rental replacement, the KPIs below let you see where the time and money are going and hold your vendors to a standard.

What HEDS Looks At

Each row is a dealer or rental KPI read from the owner's side. The target is the HEDS target for that KPI, and the last column says why it matters to you. The KPI names link to the definitions in the KPI Libraries.

Buy-Side Guidance From Product History

Every machine leaves a record in its service history, parts purchases, downtime and maintenance cost. This is how HEDS reads that record.

Start by collecting the unit's service work orders, parts purchases, down days and total maintenance cost for the period you want to judge. Work out maintenance cost as a percent of the unit's OEC, and compare it with the rest of your fleet and with the HEDS target in the table above.

Then look at the downtime and its causes, such as waiting on parts, waiting on labor, or repeat visits for the same fault. Score the vendor on the same record: the labor rate billed against the posted rate, whether the parts were on the shelf, how long the work orders took to close, and whether the first repair held.

Finally, decide to repair, rebuild, replace or buy with that history in hand, and take the vendor's own numbers to your next conversation with your dealer or rental company.

The history is the evidence, and the decision stays with you.
