Service Manager

The Service scorecard has eight core KPIs: Labor Recovery, Labor Utilization, Technician Efficiency, Effective Labor Rate, Last Labor to Invoice, Service Absorption, First-Time Fix Rate, and Service Labor Gross Margin. Labor figures come from the time clock and the work orders for the same period. Last Labor to Invoice is the 48-hour close rule: a work order is open past 48 hours after its last labor.

Core KPIs (8)

KPIFormulaHEDS Target
Labor RecoveryHours on work orders divided by paid payroll hours, times 10095% to 100%
Labor UtilizationHours invoiced on work orders divided by hours on work orders, times 100100% to 110%. Above 110% passes. 85% or below is a red flag.
Technician EfficiencyShop: flat rate or estimated time divided by actual job time, times 100. Road: billed hours divided by clocked on-job hours, times 100.Shop 110%. Road 80% to 110%.
Effective Labor RateEffective labor rate on customer-billed work divided by posted labor rate, times 10092% of posted labor rate. Below 92% is a red flag.
Last Labor to InvoiceCount of work orders not invoiced within 48 hours of the last labor posted on the work order48 hours
Service AbsorptionService department gross profit divided by the location's total operating expenses, times 100. This is service's contribution to the location's absorption.Service carries 70% to 75% of the location's absorption. Below 70% is a red flag.
First-Time Fix RateRepairs fixed on the first attempt divided by completed repairs, times 10085% to 90%
Service Labor Gross Margin(Labor sales minus direct technician cost) divided by labor sales, times 10075% to 80%

Parts Manager

The Parts scorecard has eight core KPIs. Turns are reported overall and by ABC class, since a single part can run from about one turn to thousands. Parts Aging (slow moving at 6 to 8 months, obsolete at 12 months with no movement) sits inside Obsolete and Excess Stock, and Days of Supply, a secondary measure, is read alongside it.

Core KPIs (8)

KPIFormulaHEDS Target
Parts Gross Margin(Parts sales minus parts cost of sales) divided by parts sales, times 10033% to 37%
Parts AbsorptionParts department gross profit divided by the location's total operating expenses, times 100. This is parts' contribution to the location's absorption.Parts carries 30% to 40% of the location's absorption. Below 30% is a red flag.
Gross Parts TurnsAnnual parts cost of sales divided by average parts inventory at cost3 to 4 turns a year, reported overall and by ABC class
Stock Order PercentParts purchases on stock orders divided by total parts purchases, times 10080%
Obsolete and Excess StockObsolete and excess inventory divided by total parts inventory, times 100Under 5% of parts inventory
Counter Fill RateCounter line items filled from stock divided by counter line items requested, times 10085%, the sweet spot
Service Fill RateService line items filled from stock divided by service line items requested, times 10090% to 95%
Parts Salaries as a Percent of Parts Gross ProfitParts salaries divided by parts gross profit, times 10030% or less

Secondary measures (4)

These stay in the library with their definitions. They are read as needed and are not on the core scorecard.

Location Absorption

Absorption uses the total operating expenses of the whole location. Service and parts each show their share of the same result. These measures are worked on the Service and Parts dashboards. Service Absorption is counted in the Service eight and Parts Absorption in the Parts eight, so the three are shown here once as a set.

Core KPIs (3)

KPIFormulaHEDS Target
Location AbsorptionService gross profit plus parts gross profit, divided by the location's total operating expenses, times 100The location target is 100% to 110%.
Service AbsorptionService department gross profit divided by the location's total operating expenses, times 100. This is service's contribution to the location's absorption.Service carries 70% to 75% of the location's absorption. Below 70% is a red flag.
Parts AbsorptionParts department gross profit divided by the location's total operating expenses, times 100. This is parts' contribution to the location's absorption.Parts carries 30% to 40% of the location's absorption. Below 30% is a red flag.
