Fractional Executive

Aged WIP sits in the shop and ties up cash. Technicians get paid for hours that never hit an invoice. Parts stock-outs and dead stock slow the work, rental units sit down, and branches run on different habits.

HEDS steps into a named operating seat and installs a daily rhythm so those leaks get closed and the numbers start moving.

Who This Is For

HEDS fills named fractional and interim operating seats for equipment dealers and rental companies, including construction, agricultural, truck, forklift and crane rental dealers, municipal and utility fleets, and private equity and search fund buyers of those businesses.

The seats include Interim and Fractional VP of Product Support, and Director of Service, Parts and Aftermarket Operations, for equipment dealers and rental companies, plus Fixed Operations leadership.

What the Client Gets

The operation gets a named leader for a fixed term, a clear mandate, and coaching for the managers who report in the seat. Each quote shows the hours and the cost up front. The Operational Health Analysis findings put a dollar figure on the leaks, so the owner can weigh the cost of the work against what it recovers.
