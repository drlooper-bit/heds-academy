Fractional Executive

Aged WIP sits in the shop and ties up cash. Technicians get paid for hours that never hit an invoice. Parts stock-outs and dead stock slow the work, rental units sit down, and branches run on different habits.

HEDS steps into a named operating seat and installs a daily rhythm so those leaks get closed and the numbers start moving.

The service

The seats include Interim and Fractional VP of Product Support, and Director of Service, Parts and Aftermarket Operations, for equipment dealers and rental companies, plus Fixed Operations leadership.

You and HEDS agree which seat we will fill, for how long, and which managers we will lead and coach. You agree the onsite days, the weekly cadence, and the reports that will be sent so our team can run the function with a clear mandate. HEDS leads the function for the agreed term and coaches the managers who report in it. At the end of the term you decide whether the seat ends, is extended, or is handed to a permanent leader.

What the client gets

The operation gets a named leader for a fixed term, a clear mandate, and coaching for the managers who report in the seat.

Price

The price is $200 an hour. Each service is priced from the realistic hours required to perform it, and the quote lists the hours and the total before work starts. Travel time, mileage, lodging and meals are invoiced at actual cost. There is no set package or project price. This is an example of the math, not a package: 16 hours at $200 an hour is $3,200.

Each quote shows the hours and the cost up front. The Operational Health Analysis findings put a dollar figure on the leaks, so the owner can weigh the cost of the work against what it recovers.
