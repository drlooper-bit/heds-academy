Fractional Executive

Aged WIP sits in the shop and ties up cash. Technicians get paid for hours that never hit an invoice. Parts stock-outs and dead stock slow the work, rental units sit down, and branches run on different habits.

HEDS takes a named seat in your operation and runs it. The seats are Interim and Fractional VP of Product Support, and Director of Service, Parts and Aftermarket Operations, for equipment dealers and rental companies, plus Fixed Operations leadership. We run the daily and weekly rhythm with your managers and report the numbers to you every week.

The seat is priced at $200 an hour from the realistic hours it requires, and the quote lists the hours and the total before work starts.

The seat is run on the shop floor, the parts counter and the rental yard, on a fixed rhythm your managers can see.

Who This Is For

The seat is for construction, agricultural, truck, forklift and crane rental dealers, municipal and utility fleets, and private equity and search fund buyers of those businesses. It fits when a product support or fixed operations seat is empty, when the owner keeps getting pulled back into daily fires, or when a newly bought business needs its service, parts and rental habits brought to one standard.

Before the seat starts, you and HEDS agree which seat we will fill, for how long, which managers we will lead and coach, the onsite days, the weekly cadence, and the reports that will be sent. The Operational Health Analysis findings put a dollar figure on the leaks, so you can weigh the cost of the seat against what it recovers.
